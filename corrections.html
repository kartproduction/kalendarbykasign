Report a wrong date

Kalendar’s Nepali dates and festivals are built from published calendar sources, but mistakes can still happen. Tithi, nakshatra, sunrise and sunset are calculated and approximate. If you find something wrong, please tell us.

Please include
  • The date (AD and BS if you know both).
  • What the app shows, and what it should show.
  • Where you checked it, for example a printed patro (name and year) or an official notice.
Email us the correction
This opens your email app with a short form filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send. We use your message only to check the date and, if you want, to write back.

When we confirm a mistake, we fix it in the next calendar data update, which the app downloads by itself.
