Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no accounts, subscriptions, or ads.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the website works offline, and the Android app works offline from the start.

Where is my data stored?

Settings, favorites, and events are saved on your own device and never sent to a server. See the privacy policy.

How accurate are the Nepali BS dates?

BS dates are built in for 2082–2088 BS. Dates for 2083 match published calendars. The other years come from public converter tables and are not independently checked. Festivals are listed through 2027, and Purnima, Aunsi and Ekadashi are approximate. Please check important dates against an official patro before printing or planning around them.

Do reminders work on the website?

Reminders need the Android app, because they use your phone’s notifications. On the website you can still save events and add them to your phone calendar with the .ics file.

Can I use it in Nepali?

Yes. Tap the language button in the app to switch between English and नेपाली.

What if I change phones?

Use Export backup in the app to save your events, then Import backup on the new phone. Your events are only stored on your device, so a backup is the way to keep them safe.

How do I install the Android app?

Tap Download for Android, open the file, and allow the install if Android asks. The file is the Kalendar app installer.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. Add photos in the app to try them on a design, and the project owner can bundle final photos so they load automatically.
