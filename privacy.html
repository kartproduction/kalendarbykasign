Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026.

Kalendar by Kasign has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Your settings, favorites and events are saved only on your device and are never sent to us.

The app asks for no special permissions. When you save a PNG or PDF, the app saves it to your device (Pictures or Downloads in the Android app, or your browser’s downloads on the web), or opens your device’s share sheet.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs when you visit, under its own privacy statement. The Android download page asks GitHub for the latest release information.

Photos you add inside the app are used only on your device while you work. Contact: khadanacharya@gmail.com.
