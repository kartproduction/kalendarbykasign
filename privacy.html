Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026.

In short: your events and settings stay on this phone. The app only checks online for calendar data and app updates, and never uploads your events.

Kalendar by Kasign has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Your settings, favorites and events are saved only on your device and are never sent to us.

The app asks for no special permissions. The only exception is notifications: if you turn on reminders, Android asks permission to show them. Reminders are created and shown on your device from the events you saved there, and nothing is sent to us. The Android app also checks GitHub for the newest release number so it can tell you when an update is available; this request carries no personal information. The app and the website also download a small calendar data file (Nepali month lengths and festival dates) from this website, and nothing about you is sent with the request. The optional home-screen widget shows dates stored on your phone and makes no network requests. If you tap “Send feedback”, your email app opens with a message that includes the app version and your phone model, and it goes only to the address you send it to. When you save a PNG or PDF, the app saves it to your device (Pictures or Downloads in the Android app, or your browser’s downloads on the web), or opens your device’s share sheet.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs when you visit, under its own privacy statement. The Android download page asks GitHub for the latest release information.

Photos you add inside the app are used only on your device while you work. Contact: khadanacharya@gmail.com.
